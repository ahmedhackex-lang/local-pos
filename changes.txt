Looking at your actual project structure, I would **not rewrite everything**.

You have a common trap:

```text
MVP Working
    ↓
Architect gets excited
    ↓
Rewrites everything
    ↓
Never ships
```

```text

2. Frontend architecture
3. Database choice
4. Future maintainability
```
